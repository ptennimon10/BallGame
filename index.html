    How to play

    1. Enter your name. You need one to play. It shows on the leaderboard.
    2. Pick a ball. Every ball has its own power. Powers fire by themselves during the fight.
    3. Aim. Before the timer runs out, tap or drag in the arena to point your arrow, then press Lock Aim.
    4. Watch it bounce. Balls bounce off the walls and each other. Every bump hurts both balls.
    5. Pop the other ball. Knock its HP to 0 to win the round. Lose a round and you lose a heart. Run out of hearts and you lose.

    Balls speed up the longer a round goes. After 45 seconds it's overtime: both balls lose HP and nobody can heal. When both sides are down to their last life, it's sudden death.

    Wins earn coins for new balls, trails and auras. Once you own both 150-coin balls, match rewards drop to 70%, so save up. Daily rewards and quests always pay in full.
