      How to play

      1. Enter your name. You need one to play. It shows on the leaderboard and in online games.
      2. Pick a ball. Every ball has its own power. Balls marked ⚡ have a power you fire yourself. The rest work on their own.
      3. Aim. Before the timer runs out, tap or drag in the arena to point your arrow, then press Lock Aim.
      4. Take control. During the fight, tap the arena to send your ball toward that spot. You can turn every 2 seconds. Press POWER (or E) to use your power when it's charged. Q turns you straight at the nearest enemy.
      5. Pop the other ball. Every bump hurts both balls. Knock its HP to 0 to win the round. Lose a round and you lose a heart.
      6. Switch balls between rounds. Pick from 3 balls you own. 1 reroll per round costs 5 coins.

      Balls keep speeding up the longer a round goes. After 45 seconds it's overtime: both balls lose HP and nobody can heal. When both sides are down to their last life, it's sudden death. Prefer the old style? Set Powers to auto at the top of the menu.

      Every win pays 50 coins in any mode. Losses pay a few coins too. New balls come from the Gumball Machine in the Shop, one level at a time: collect every Rare ball to unlock the Epic machine, then Mythic, then Legendary. Tap 🪙 on a ball you own to upgrade its health, damage, speed or ability, or to buy its Hypercharge (fill the meter, then press H or HYPER). Upgrades and Hypercharges don't count in Ranked.

      On the Balls tab: the Fusion Lab mixes two balls you own into a new one with both powers (you keep both), and the Custom Workshop lets you design your own ball with any power you own for 1,000 coins.

      Every 5 seconds the walls close in a little, so stalling doesn't work. Deal damage to fill your MUTATION meter: when it's full your ball transforms for 6 seconds (Blaze becomes Inferno, Frost becomes Glacier and so on). Watch for combos: fire on poison goo makes a toxic flame, ice on a stuck or inked ball shatters it, and explosions clear goo, puddles and peels.

      Open Profile to see your stats and pick a title. On the Social tab, add friends with their player ID: friends who add each other can message and invite each other, and you can make group chats with a code. Hosts can make their game public so anyone online can join. Ranked uses Elo: beating a higher-ranked player wins more trophies. Goals give coins, titles and exclusive trails and auras. Ranked seasons last 4 weeks: finish high for season rewards, then trophies are halved for the next season.

      Spin the wheel twice every 24 hours for coins, trails or auras. The 1,500-coin Jackpot is 1 in 500. One Limited ball, Nova, can't be bought or won at all. It needs a secret code, and there are more secret codes hidden around the game for limited balls and rewards.
